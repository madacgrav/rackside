# Support

Need help with Rackside? Check the questions below or the [user guide](guide/). If you're still stuck, get in touch:

- **Email:** [madacgrav@gmail.com](mailto:madacgrav@gmail.com)
- **Report a bug or request a feature:** [GitHub issues](https://github.com/madacgrav/rackside/issues)

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the Rackside version shown in **Settings › About**.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. Rackside has no account and no cloud sync. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

### How do I back up my data?
Go to **Settings › Backup › Export all data…** and save the file somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive or an email to yourself. Rackside shows a badge on Settings and a banner on Schedule when it's time to export again. An export file is the only way to recover your data if your iPhone is lost or broken.

### I have a new iPhone, or I reinstalled Rackside. Where is my data?
Your purchase carries over — download Rackside again from the App Store at no charge — but your data does not. On the new install, go to **Settings › Backup › Import from file…** and choose your most recent export. Without an export file, data from the old install can't be recovered.

### Rackside is locked and Face ID isn't working.
Tap **Unlock**. If Face ID fails, iOS lets you enter your iPhone passcode instead.

### Rackside says it "couldn't open its data store".
Tap **Restore last backup** first — Rackside keeps a copy of your data from before the last update. If that doesn't work, **Start fresh (then import an export file)** clears the app so you can import your latest export from Settings.

### My Apple Watch doesn't show the timer.
Open Rackside on your iPhone and start a session. The watch app gets its timer from your iPhone, so keep your iPhone nearby with Bluetooth on.

### I'm not getting session reminders.
Check **Settings › Permissions** in Rackside. If Notifications shows as denied, tap **Open Settings** and allow notifications for Rackside.

### Sessions aren't appearing in my calendar.
Turn on **Add to iPhone calendar** when you create the session, and allow calendar access in **Settings › Permissions**. Events go to your default calendar.
