# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 1 November 2026

## Summary

TrainMe does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone unless you choose to send it somewhere.

## What TrainMe stores, and where

TrainMe stores the information you enter in the app's private storage on your iPhone. That includes:

- **Clients:** name, mobile number, goals, injuries and limitations, notes, date of birth, occupation, training history, availability, emergency contact and physician details.
- **Health screening:** answers to the screening questions, conditions, surgeries, medications, family heart history, symptoms, sleep, stress, tobacco and alcohol use, activity level, and whether medical clearance was received.
- **Assessments:** measurements such as resting heart rate, blood pressure, height, weight, girths, body fat, push-ups and plank time, and movement-assessment observations.
- **Waivers:** the waiver text sent to each client and the dates it was sent and returned. TrainMe does not store signatures.
- **Training:** exercises, workout templates, scheduled sessions, session notes and every recorded set.
- **Settings:** your unit and timer preferences and when you last exported.

This data is protected by iOS Data Protection while your iPhone is locked, and TrainMe can additionally require Face ID (or your passcode) to open. TrainMe also keeps an automatic backup copy on your iPhone before an app update changes how data is stored.

## When information leaves your iPhone

Only when you ask it to:

- **Calendar:** if you turn on *Add to iPhone calendar* for a session, TrainMe creates a calendar event with the client's name, the time and the session notes. Your calendar may sync that event to other services you use (for example iCloud or Google).
- **Text reminders:** TrainMe can open a pre-filled text message to a client. You review it and tap Send yourself.
- **Reports and waivers:** progress reports (PDF or image) and waiver PDFs are shared through the iOS share sheet to wherever you choose.
- **Export files:** *Export all data…* creates a file with all of your TrainMe data and saves it where you choose (for example iCloud Drive or Files). Keep it somewhere safe — it contains your clients' information.
- **Apple Watch:** your iPhone sends the watch only the running timer and the name and length of the next timed exercise. No client information is sent to the watch.

## Notifications

Session reminders and timer alerts are local notifications created and delivered on your devices. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Permissions

- **Calendar:** used only to add, update and remove the sessions you choose to put in your calendar. TrainMe does not read your other events.
- **Face ID:** used only to unlock TrainMe. Apple handles Face ID; TrainMe never sees your face data.
- **Notifications:** used for session reminders (24 hours and 1 hour before) and timer alerts.

You can change these at any time in the iOS Settings app.

## Your clients' information

You are responsible for obtaining your clients' consent to record their information and for handling it in line with the laws and professional rules that apply to you. TrainMe is a record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice; the health screening is not a diagnosis.

## Deleting your data

Deleting TrainMe from your iPhone deletes all of its data. Export files, shared reports and calendar events you created live outside the app; delete them where you saved them.

## Children

TrainMe is intended for fitness professionals and is not directed to children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
