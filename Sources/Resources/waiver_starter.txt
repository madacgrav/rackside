PERSONAL TRAINING WAIVER, RELEASE OF LIABILITY, AND ASSUMPTION OF RISK

PARTICIPATION
I want to take part in personal training sessions, fitness assessments, and exercise programs with my trainer ("the Trainer"). I take part voluntarily.

ASSUMPTION OF RISK
!! I understand that exercise and physical training involve risks, including muscle strains and sprains, joint and back injuries, falls, heart attack, stroke, and in rare cases death. I knowingly and voluntarily accept all of these risks, whether known or unknown, including risks arising from the ordinary negligence of the Trainer.

RELEASE AND WAIVER
!! To the fullest extent allowed by law, I release, waive, and agree not to sue the Trainer, and anyone acting for the Trainer, for any injury, loss, or damage connected with my training, including claims based on the Trainer's ordinary negligence. This release does not cover gross negligence or intentional misconduct.

MEDICAL ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
I have answered the health screening honestly and completely. I will tell the Trainer right away about any change in my health, medications, or injuries. If the screening recommends medical clearance, I will get it from my physician before training. I will stop exercising and tell the Trainer if I feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, or shortness of breath.

EMERGENCY TREATMENT CONSENT
If I need emergency care during a session, I consent to the Trainer calling emergency services and my emergency contact. I am responsible for the cost of any treatment I receive.

ENTIRE AGREEMENT AND SEVERABILITY
This document is the entire agreement about the risks of training and replaces any earlier statements. If any part is found unenforceable, the rest stays in effect.

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
I have read this document, I understand it, and I sign it voluntarily. I understand that I am giving up legal rights by signing.
